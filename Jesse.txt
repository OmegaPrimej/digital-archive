The specific term for when a property is listed without a public address is a **"pocket listing"** or **"private exclusive listing"**.

The goal is to create a legal and financial barrier between the public-facing property records and the actual individual. Let me break down the two primary vehicles used, based on what you're describing.

### 🏛️ Land Trusts: The Original "Blind" Purchase

This is a very traditional method. In this setup, the property's title is held by a trust, and a third party (usually a lawyer or a fiduciary) is named as the **trustee**. Public land records will show the trustee's name, not the celebrity's. The true owner is the **beneficiary** of the trust, and that information is kept private in the trust agreement, which is not publicly recorded. This is a classic approach for pure privacy.

### 🏢 LLCs: The Modern Veil of Privacy

This is the more common and versatile method used today. A celebrity (or their advisor) will form a Limited Liability Company (LLC) with a generic name that doesn't point back to them—something like "Blue Sky Holdings LLC" or even using the property's street number. The LLC is then listed as the legal owner on all public documents, including the deed, tax records, and any public listings.

*   **The Delaware/Wyoming Advantage**: To further strengthen this veil, the LLC is often formed in a state like Delaware or Wyoming, which have strong privacy laws that don't require the names of the LLC's members (owners) or managers to be publicly disclosed.

### 🎭 The Layered Approach & "Blind Trusts"

For the highest level of privacy, these two strategies are combined. A Trust will be created to own and manage an LLC, which in turn holds the title to the property. This creates multiple layers, making it extremely difficult for anyone to trace the ownership back to an individual. You'll also hear the term "Blind Trust" used in this context. In a true blind trust for real estate, the beneficiary (the celebrity) gives up control over the asset and may not even know the specific details of the transaction, which is managed entirely by an independent trustee to prevent any potential conflicts of interest.

### 🤫 Private "Pocket" Listings and NDAs

The anonymity often starts before the sale is even public. High-profile properties are frequently sold as **"pocket listings"** or **"private exclusive listings."** This means the property is not put on the public Multiple Listing Service (MLS). Instead, it's marketed discreetly through private broker networks to pre-vetted, qualified buyers. Everyone involved, from agents to potential buyers, is typically required to sign a strict Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA) to ensure the transaction stays confidential.
